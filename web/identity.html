ProveML · EU Digital Identity Wallet PID

A model writes on top of a credential. Every claim resolves to a disclosed attribute.

The fact store is a Person Identification Data credential in the EUDI format (SD-JWT VC, urn:eudi:pid:1). The holder discloses what they choose, with key binding to this verifier's nonce; the relying party checks the issuer signature and the digests. A summary drafted by a model is then checked claim by claim against what was disclosed, and nothing else.

What this does not do. It verifies that the text matches the disclosed attributes, not that the person is who the credential says: that is the issuer's signature, which the relying party checked before any text was written. Nor that the name shown is the only person with that name: the verifier checks the name against the id the model chose and reports whether that name is unique in the store; here there is one presentation and one person, and in a bank the disambiguator is the credential's digest, not the name. The PID carries no age; the relying party derives it from the birth date, and that derivation is a fact with its own proof line, not something the verifier computes.

    
Format: SD-JWT VC per the PID Rulebook v1.7; library: OpenWallet Foundation sd-jwt-js. The credential is issued by a demo provider key; member-state wallets are due by the end of 2026.
